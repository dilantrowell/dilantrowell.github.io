Project journey · Project Lead

AutoBridge OT–IT Integration

A pilot that had to prove itself, then grow into a production system. The story of building a bridge between the shop floor and the enterprise.

01

Two worlds, one question

Shop-floor systems and enterprise systems spoke different languages. I started by getting both sides in one room to agree what a single source of truth would actually mean.

02

Prove it small

I scoped a pilot with a clear finish line, so the idea could be judged on evidence rather than opinion.

03

Let the pilot earn its place

I reviewed the pilot results with stakeholders, built the case for production, and was honest about what the pilot would never do.

04

Scale with the external team

The pilot plan became a production roadmap. I coordinated the external developers and the people who run the line so both moved together.

05

Light up the KPIs

Real-time KPI dashboards switched on. I tracked whether people used them, not only whether we delivered them.
