FIFO System for RM & FG Stores

First in, first out sounds simple. Try it below, then read how I made it stick in two stores.

FIFO compliance80% → 95% in two months
01

Measure first

I started with systematic inventory monitoring, so we argued about facts instead of feelings.

02

Fix the data

Batch records had errors that made FIFO impossible to follow. Correcting them was unglamorous and essential.

03

Tighten the door

Stronger controls at material receiving, so the problem stopped re-entering the store.

04

Keep it visible

Compliance was tracked openly. It rose from 80% to 95% within two months.

05

Finish the last mile

100% FIFO on every raw material except one. I am still working on that one.

What stayed with me

Rules hold when the process makes the right thing the easy thing.
