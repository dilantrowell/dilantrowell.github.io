Project journey · Project Lead

AutoBridge 2W Barcode Management System

Follow one tyre down the line. Every scan gate below is a decision I had to make with the people who work there.

01

Start where the tyre begins

I started at tyre building, walking the floor to understand how a tyre gets its identity and what goes wrong when that identity is lost.

02

Every role, one flow

I listed each shop-floor role and what it needed from a scan, so the system fitted the job instead of the other way round.

03

Design the hand-offs

Working with the external development team, I agreed where the scan points sit between stages and what counts as a clean hand-off.

04

Close at final inspection

Connecting the last checkpoint meant a tyre's trail could finally run from start to finish without gaps.

05

Go live on the floor

Training and support at go-live, then watching manual reconciliation effort and error rates come down.

What stayed with me

Shop-floor adoption is decided by how little extra effort a scan costs.
