Laboratory Test Data Management System

Drag the slider to see the change: test results compiled by hand on one side, a live view on the other. Below it, how I got there.

Before · compiled by hand
After · live dashboard
Step 1

Learn the lab day

I spent time with the lab team to see how results were captured, checked and shared, before proposing anything.

Step 2

Agree the data

Together we decided which results matter, how they are named, and who owns each one.

Step 3

Coordinate the teams

Many teams touch the data. I kept their priorities aligned so the delivery plan stayed realistic.

Step 4

Go live

End-to-end delivery and go-live, with the cross-functional teams ready on the day.

Step 5

Hand it to decisions

Manual reporting gave way to automated dashboards that support real-time decisions.

What stayed with me

When results arrive in real time, the conversation changes from "what happened" to "what do we do".
