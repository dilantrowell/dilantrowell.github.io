Digital Invoice Reconciliation & Document Management

Follow one invoice from the moment it arrives to the moment it is safely stored. The project is still in progress, and this is the plan I am steering.

In progress
01

Follow one invoice

I traced a single invoice from arrival to storage, noting every place it sat waiting for a person. That map became the project scope.

02

Digitise it

OCR-based capture turns paper into data. With the finance team I agreed which fields matter, so we capture what we use and nothing extra.

03

Reconcile with SAP

Automated matching against SAP handles the routine cases. I am designing how the unusual ones reach a human, with the right context.

04

Store it securely

Document management with proper access control, so an invoice is easy to find for the right person and invisible to everyone else.

05

Trace it end to end

Every invoice carries a trail from receipt to secure storage. That trail is what makes the whole thing auditable and trustworthy.
