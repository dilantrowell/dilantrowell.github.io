Project journey · Project Co-Lead, Cross-Functional Team

CEAT Connect Mobile App

From a blank screen to the Google Play Store and the Apple App Store. Scroll and watch the phone fill in as the project moves.

01

Concept

It started with a plain question: what do dealers actually need in their pocket? I aligned a cross-functional team around one clear purpose before anyone opened a design tool.

02

Design together

Rough sketches went to stakeholders early. Changing a sketch costs minutes; changing a built screen costs weeks.

03

Build in the open

As co-lead I kept scope, progress and trade-offs visible to everyone, so no one was surprised by what the app would and would not do at launch.

04

Test with real users

Before submitting to the stores, the app went through proper testing, and I tracked every issue to a decision, not just a fix.

05

Public launch

Live on the Google Play Store and the Apple App Store. I handled the store requirements and the launch checklist with the team.
