Project journey · Project Lead

Enterprise Data Warehousing & BI Platform

From reports stitched together by hand to one platform where the numbers arrive on their own. This is how I led it, step by step.

01

Spot the pain

Reports were being assembled by hand across several systems. Before touching any tool, I sat with the people who built and read them to learn where the time really went.

02

Frame the scope

Many report requests became one platform scope. With the external development team I agreed what was in, what could wait, and how we would know it worked.

03

Connect the sources

I brought the owners of SAP S/4HANA and the Sales and Marketing systems together, so each data feed had a named owner and one agreed definition.

04

Build in slices

Regular checkpoints with the external team. Users reviewed early dashboards before anything was polished, so surprises came early and cheap.

05

Retire the manual

Dashboards went live and manual reporting was switched off. I made sure everyone knew where to look and who to ask.
